Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your Lake Kivu adventure.

📅 Booking & Availability
How do I book?+

Send Tom a WhatsApp at +250 791 750 041 or fill the online booking form. He confirms within hours.

How far in advance?+

1–2 days for individuals. Groups of 10+ should book at least 1 week ahead. Same-day sometimes possible.

Do you operate weekends?+

Yes! Every day including weekends and public holidays, 7AM to 7PM.

Can I book same day?+

Yes if Tom has availability. WhatsApp as early in the morning as possible to check.

💰 Pricing & Payment
Cheapest activity?+

Kayaking at $50/hour. Life jacket and safety briefing included. No experience needed.

Are prices all-inclusive?+

Yes. All prices include life jackets, safety briefing, and professional supervision. No hidden fees.

Group discounts?+

Groups of 5+ get 10% off. Groups of 10+ get 15% off. Contact Tom to discuss rates.

When do I pay?+

Payment on arrival in cash (USD or RWF). MTN MoMo and Airtel Money also accepted. Deposits for large groups.

🛡️ Safety
Safe for beginners?+

Absolutely. Tom provides a full safety briefing before every activity. Life jackets are mandatory. Many first-timers join us.

Can children join?+

Yes. Kids welcome on kayaking and boat rides with a parent. Jet ski children under 12 must ride with an adult.

Bad weather?+

Tom monitors weather daily. If unsafe, he cancels and gives a full refund or free reschedule.

What to bring?+

Comfortable clothes, sunscreen, sunglasses, hat, water, camera. Tom provides all safety gear.

📍 Location & Getting There
Where do we meet?+

Lake Kivu shoreline in Rubavu (Gisenyi). Tom sends exact GPS coordinates via WhatsApp after booking.

How far from Kigali?+

About 2.5–3 hours by private car, or 3–4 hours by public bus. Beautiful drive through Rwanda's green hills.

Hotel pickup?+

Tom can arrange Rubavu hotel pickup. Ask when booking. Small fee may apply for distant hotels.

Work with hotels/agents?+

Yes! We partner with hotels and tour operators. Contact Tom about partnership arrangements.

Still Have Questions?

Tom is available daily 7AM–7PM.
